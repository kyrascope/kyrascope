2021 gave me the gift of exploration without obligation. No need to film, no need to turn every trip into content. Just being somewhere and noticing it.

That year, I started photographing Delhi NCR differently. Not for YouTube, but for the joy of it. Photography became a way to slow down and really see the place I'd lived my entire life.

There was Chandni Chowk in the early morning, before the crowds descended. I'd never actually been there without a camera crew. Walking through those narrow lanes without worrying about shots or lighting, I noticed things I'd overlooked: the way sunlight caught on brass pots, how vendors greeted each other, the rhythm of the market waking up.

We'd spend afternoons in Lodhi Gardens. I'd bring a sketchbook and just sit. Watching birds, observing how people moved through the space, drawing trees. My sketches were terrible, but the act of trying to capture something made me see it more clearly.

I discovered cafes in Khan Market and Hauz Khas with my friends. This was new—having favorite coffee spots, being a regular somewhere, just existing as an eleven-year-old and not as a content creator. Small moments. A corner table where sunlight came in perfectly. A barista who remembered our order.

I took the metro alone for the first time that year, which felt like a rite of passage. I documented the experience through photos—the colorful seats, the diversity of commuters, the chaos and order of public transport.

One day, Dad took me to his favorite bookstore in Greater Kailash. I'd been there before, but I'd never really been there. We spent hours wandering the stacks, and I picked books based purely on what interested me. It felt like opening up a new part of myself.

I photographed monsoon—rain on our verandah, the way the city looked after a downpour, puddles reflecting buildings. I photographed our neighborhood in different seasons, watching how the same trees transformed.

I documented my school, my friends' faces, the uniform corridor where we spent hours together. These will be memories I treasure—before everything gets complicated, before life accelerates further.

Delhi NCR is home. That year, I finally saw it not as a backdrop for videos, but as the actual place where my life happens. And through photography and presence, I fell in love with it all over again.
